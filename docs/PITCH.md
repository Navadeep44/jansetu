# Pitch outline (8 slides)

**Hackathon:** Track 1: AI for Digital Public Infrastructure & Governance · Theme: **Innovation**.

1. **Title.** JanSetu: every voice, every Indian language, the right public investment.
2. **Problem, through people.** Lakshmi (Narnoor, Adilabad; Telugu / Gondi; no phone; no all-weather road, the ambulance
   can't reach). Ramesh (Bawana, Delhi; Bhojpuri; told "grievance disposed", drain still floods). Sunita (Mihinpurwa,
   Bahraich; Hindi; no doctor; nobody from her block complains). Priya (Malakpet, Hyderabad; Telugu; daily power cuts).
   Facts: only 28.4% of rural women own a phone; over 95% "disposal" with many formulaic closures; CAG findings on MPLADS.
3. **Insight.** Filing grievances is solved: **CPGRAMS** and its voice bot **Samadhan Didi** (May 2026, 22 languages) handle
   each grievance as a **ticket** to close. Nobody turns those voices into **development planning**: demand → need gap →
   investment → impact.
4. **Solution.** Five steps: Listen (voice, WhatsApp, IVR, ASHA, Gram Sabha), Understand and merge (13 Indian languages,
   households not messages), Fuse with data (Mission Antyodaya, Census, NFHS-5, JJM, PMGSY), Recommend (costed projects,
   budget planner in ₹ crore), Close the loop (citizen confirms, impact measured). Screenshot: India-at-a-glance state cards
   and the silent-area map.
5. **For national and state policymakers.** State cards with drill-down to districts, silent areas, money going to low-need
   places (Banjara Hills beautification vs tribal blocks without water), **Ask** in Hindi or Telugu, printable briefs.
6. **Fits India's planning cycle.** One click drafts a **Viksit Gram Panchayat Plan** under **VB-GRAMG** (which replaced
   MGNREGA from 1 July 2026) for the Gram Sabha, with a CSV ready for **Yuktdhara**. Scheme convergence: PMGSY, JJM, SBM-G,
   AMRUT 2.0, NHM, Samagra Shiksha, RDSS, PM-KUSUM.
7. **Built as a Digital Public Good.** All 9 DPG indicators, Apache-2.0, Open311, DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025, data stays
   in India, runs offline with no keys, Bhashini-ready. UI in English, Hindi and Telugu with a Listen button on every page.
8. **Impact and ask.** Demo: complaints fell 65–84% after 4 completed projects (difference-in-differences). Ask: a pilot in
   one district per state (Adilabad, Koraput, Gaya, Bahraich, a Delhi district), CPGRAMS import, and DPG registry submission.
