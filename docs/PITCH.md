# Pitch outline (8 slides)

1. **Title.** JanSetu: every voice, every language, the right public investment. BRICS 2026, Innovation pillar: AI + DPI.
2. **Problem, through people.** Lakshmi (no phone, Telugu / Gondi, no road, ambulance can't reach), Ramesh ("grievance disposed", drain still floods), Thandi (power cuts leading to protests), Maria (rich areas get fixed first). Data: 28.4% rural women phone ownership; more than 95% "disposal" yet formulaic closures; CAG findings on MPLADS; 18 lakh GPDPs not linked to grievances.
3. **Insight.** Voice filing is solved (Samadhan Didi, May 2026). What is missing is the layer from complaints to **demand** to **investment** to **impact**.
4. **Solution.** Five-step flow: Listen, Understand & merge, Fuse with data, Recommend, Close the loop. Screenshot of the dashboard with the silent-zone layer.
5. **Differentiators.** Need-Gap Index (transparent weights), silent zones, household-level counting and anti-gaming, explainable costed projects with scheme convergence, budget optimiser, misaligned-spending detector, citizen-verified closure, DiD impact.
6. **Built as a Digital Public Good.** All 9 DPGA indicators, Apache-2.0, Open311, runs offline with no keys, Bhashini-ready, any LLM. Federated BRICS nodes with k-anonymity.
7. **Traction path.** Pilot: one district per BRICS country (Adilabad, São Paulo periphery, Johannesburg); integrate CPGRAMS / SP156 via connectors; feed GPDP / PPA / IDP planning cycles; NDB uses the aggregate exchange for lending priorities.
8. **Impact & ask.** In the demo, complaints fell 71–85% after 4 completed projects (DiD verified). Ask: a pilot partnership plus DPGA registry submission.
